12 / METHODOLOGY

Spatial analysis framework

Road network: Major and minor road geometry from GitHub.

Administrative geography: Gambia Census Enumeration Areas.

Proximity: Straight-line buffers around verified transport stops.

Road length: Turf.js geometry length calculations.

Area selection: Administrative attributes detected from EA data.

Population proxy: Population associated with EAs intersecting stop buffers, where population attributes exist. This is not a precise population coverage measure.
